Real findings, real fixes

Not just problems — solutions

Every finding comes with a plain-English explanation and step-by-step instructions to fix it yourself. No technical degree required.

Critical

No meta description on homepage

Google writes its own snippet when this is missing, resulting in lower click-through rates from search results.

Fix: Go to your SEO plugin (Yoast or RankMath) → Edit homepage snippet → Paste in the pre-written description we provide. Takes 5 minutes.
Critical

No call-to-action button above the fold

Visitors land on your site with no obvious next step. They have to scroll before they can find out how to contact you.

Fix: Add a "Book Now" or "Contact Us" button to your hero section, linked to your contact page. We show you exactly where in your theme editor.
Warning

Mobile performance score: 56/100

Google's mobile simulation flags slow image loading and render-blocking scripts. This affects your search ranking even if your site feels fast on your own phone.

Fix: Install a free image compression plugin (Smush or ShortPixel) and enable lazy loading. We walk you through both steps.
Good

SSL certificate active — Best Practices score: 100/100

Site loads securely over HTTPS with no mixed content warnings. Perfect score on Google's Best Practices audit. No action needed.
